Revamp the address page. The header shows the category of the address and its first and last blob transactions. The Transactions tab shows the sent and the received transactions together. The new Analytics tab has six sections: Activity, Transactions, Blobs, Blob Usage, Fees and Network. Each section shows metric cards and charts of the address as sender and as receiver, and a help icon explains each card and chart. The Activity section shows the age, the active days, the longest streak and the longest gap of the address, and a calendar of its daily transactions in the last year.
